Vertical 02 • Urban Rehabilitation

SRA Projects

"Structured Development. Community-Focused Outcomes."

Slum Rehabilitation Authority (SRA) schemes in Mumbai are government-regulated frameworks that replace dense, informal settlements with permanent, dignified housing while liberating valuable land for modern residential and commercial development. AI Realty focuses on SRA project opportunities and execution, navigating complex social dynamics and statutory procedures with professional integrity.

01

Assessment

Thorough evaluation of land survey, slum cluster eligibility, demographic data, and planning regulations under SRA guidelines.

02

Planning

Preparation of architectural master layout, rehabilitation density mapping, and sale-component viability modeling.

03

Coordination

Active stakeholder alignment, consent generation, cooperative society formation, and regulatory filing with SRA authorities.

04

Execution

Seamless transit accommodation or rent distribution, site clearance, statutory approvals (LOI, IOA), and civil works launch.

05

Development

High-quality rehabilitation housing delivery alongside premium commercial or residential sale tower completion.

Structured Roadmap

The 6-Phase Society Redevelopment Journey

Stage 1 01

Society Discussion

Engaging with managing committees and housing society members to understand specific aspirations, space requirements, and collective priorities.

Stage 2 02

Feasibility & Planning

Detailed architectural feasibility, allowable FSI / TDR calculations, structural review, and financial viability reports tailored to the society plot.

Stage 3 03

Project Structuring

Formulating clear commercial terms: additional carpet area, hardship corpus funds, monthly rent allowance, and bank guarantee protections.

Stage 4 04

Approvals & Coordination

Navigating municipal sanctions (MCGM / BMC), fire NOCs, environmental clearances, and formal Development Agreement execution.

Stage 5 05

Execution

Disciplined construction management, transparent progress reporting to society members, and quality control benchmarks.

Stage 6 06

Transformation

Timely handover of contemporary, amenity-rich homes to existing residents, culminating in modern landmark living.
